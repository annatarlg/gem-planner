How to use
  1. Inventory: open Inventory, find each gem you own in the checklist, click Add, then set its stars, rank, % and quantity with the buttons. Tick Awake on gems you have already awakened.
  2. Gem power: enter your loose (unallocated) gem power.
  3. Plan: click Pick main gems + plan max resonance. Each gear card shows its main gem, the gems for its resonant slots, and a green check on correct partner gems.
  4. Adjust: pick gems by hand in any slot (they lock), tick Keep to hold a main gem, and reserve ability gems under Aux.
  5. Back up: your plan saves in this browser only. Use Export backup to keep a copy, and Import backup on another device.
Locked slots are kept.

Each progressive step costs one rank 1 copy plus the GP shown. Ranks without a row are estimated in a straight line toward the next rank (marked "estimated").
